How to play

One tap makes both characters jump at once. Jump over the obstacles on the ground, run under the hanging ones without jumping. If either one hits an obstacle, the game is over.

Getting my scores back

Trophies are never erased. If you've erased your best score and games, there are two ways to get them back:

  1. In the game, open Settings, then tap Restore (Data section).
  2. If you just erased your scores, choose Erased games: the game keeps a copy.
  3. Otherwise, choose From a file, then the file nine-to-hero-parties.json. If someone sent it to you in a message, first save it to the Files app (Share button, then “Save to Files”).
  4. Tap Add. The games are added to the ones already there, without replacing or counting anything twice, and your best score is recalculated.

To keep a backup, use Share my history in Scores and store the file wherever you like.
